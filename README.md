# Korvia Compass

Create a premium, modern, highly professional business website for a company called:

KORVIA PARTNERS

Tagline:
"Your Problem. Our Solution."

Core positioning:
Korvia Partners is a business solutions and technology partner that helps businesses of every size solve practical business problems using technology, software, AI, automation, digital marketing, and consulting.

We work with:
• Local / Small Businesses
• Growing Businesses
• Startups
• SMEs
• Manufacturing Businesses
• Professional Services
• Large Enterprises

The company should NOT look like a generic web-development agency.

The core message should be:

"You focus on your business. We handle the technology."

A visitor should immediately understand:
1. Korvia listens to business problems.
2. Korvia understands the requirement.
3. Korvia proposes a practical solution.
4. Korvia builds/implements it.
5. Korvia supports and improves it.
6. Korvia helps the business grow.

DESIGN DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Create a premium corporate website inspired by modern companies such as:
Stripe, HubSpot, Accenture, Deloitte Digital, Salesforce and modern SaaS companies.

Do NOT copy any of their designs.

Visual style:
• Premium
• Clean
• Modern
• Trustworthy
• Technology-driven
• Business-focused
• Human
• Professional
• Conversion-oriented

Color palette:

Primary:
Deep Navy #071B3A

Secondary:
Dark Blue #0D2A52

Accent:
Korvia Orange #FF6B00

Background:
White / very light blue-gray

Text:
Dark Navy / charcoal

Use orange only for important highlights, buttons and emphasis.

Typography:
Use a modern geometric sans-serif font such as Inter, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans or similar.

Design characteristics:
• Large typography
• Spacious sections
• Rounded cards
• Subtle shadows
• Soft gradients
• Clean icons
• Subtle animations
• Modern illustrations
• Professional business photography
• Strong visual hierarchy

Avoid:
• Cheap template appearance
• Excessive gradients
• Too many animations
• Generic stock imagery
• Overly futuristic cyberpunk design
• Excessive glassmorphism
• Too many colors
• Cluttered layouts

The website must feel like a company that can serve both a small local business and a large enterprise.
